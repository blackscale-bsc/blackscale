Questions

Why do I have to post first?

Because the free dragon is the announcement. One post per free claim — verified by the gate, enforced by the contract. Paid mints skip it entirely.

Can I claim the free one twice?

No. One per wallet, and one X account can only power one claim — both are checked on-chain.

When do I see my dragon?

At reveal, when the mint window closes. Everyone's box opens at the same moment. Until then you hold the sealed box — and you can trade it on the built-in market.

What happens to unminted dragons?

They are never created. When the window closes, minting is closed for good.
